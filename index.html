🎙 Voice practice

Speak, get a transcript, then have it critiqued like written work. Record in short turns (30–90s) — critique of a long monologue collapses into vague advice, while turn-sized chunks give you specific, fixable corrections. Audio is held in memory only and dropped the moment it's transcribed; nothing is ever stored. Can't find the words? Speak in Malay, then translate to English or Arabic and use the result as your script.

Sheet & AI connection

Paste the Web App URL from your Apps Script deployment, and a free Gemini API key for the Ask AI / sentence analysis features. Both are saved on this device for next time.

Stops anyone who discovers your Web App URL from reading or writing your sheet. Must match SHARED_SECRET in phrase-trainer-sheet-sync.gs exactly, or be blank on both sides.

Important: Google's free limits are per project, not per key. Make each key with Create API key in new project in AI Studio (or from a different Google account) — two keys from the same project share one quota and won't help. When a key or model hits its limit, the app moves to the next one automatically. Daily limits reset at midnight Pacific time.
